How to use

Three steps to your softest skin

  1. 01

    Shower first

    Cleanse as normal. Leave skin slightly damp before stepping out. Do not pat fully dry. This is when your skin is most ready to absorb.

  2. 02

    Apply while damp

    Scoop a small amount and massage it in circular motions. The warmth of your hands melts it in perfectly. Focus on knees, elbows, and anywhere that tends to dry out fast.

  3. 03

    Let it absorb

    Give it 60 seconds, then dress normally. Your skin holds onto that moisture for hours without any greasy feeling. Repeat daily for best results.
